# Screenshot Brief

Write this before composing or exporting a serious screenshot set.

## Required Format

```markdown
# Screenshot Brief

Audience:
Target store or channel:
Export targets:
Card count:
Narrative arc:

## Source States

| Card | Purpose | Required app state | Existing capture | Missing scenario |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Template Direction

| Card | Template family | Why this template | Copy intent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Capture Gaps

- [ ] Missing state:
- [ ] Suggested `shotation.registry.json` scenario:

## Validation Plan

- Commands:
- Image checks:
- Manifest checks:
```

## Decision Checklist

- Audience is specific enough to guide language and source states.
- Store/channel constraints are named: Apple, Google Play, marketing, social, or web.
- Card count matches the story. Do not add cards just to use every capture.
- Narrative moves from value to proof, then depth or trust.
- Each card has one main idea.
- Missing captures are stated as app states, not vague wishes.
- Suggested registry scenarios identify the exact state to launch or seed.
- Export targets are explicit enough to verify dimensions and paths.
