# Shotation Template Playbook

Use templates by card purpose, not by visual taste alone. A strong set usually moves from promise, to proof, to workflow, to depth, to trust.

## App Store Core

- Hero promise: first card, one strongest product claim, one primary source state. Needs a clean home or core-value capture.
- Raw UI proof: when the interface itself is the proof. Needs a high-quality source capture with minimal cropping.
- Workflow sequence: show two or three states in order. Needs related captures from the same workflow.
- Feature stack: make one feature feel deeper with metadata, callouts, or supporting proof. Needs a primary capture plus one or two supporting facts.
- Social proof: use a quote, review, team/customer proof, or visible trust result. Needs a proof source or credible text.

## App Store Additions

- Comparison: use when the product beats a baseline or old flow. Needs two comparable source states or one source plus a clear baseline label.
- Before/after: use for improvement, cleanup, coaching, editing, or generated output. Needs before and after states.
- Benefit grid: use for multiple lightweight benefits. Needs three or four concrete proof points.
- Checklist: use for setup, readiness, compliance, or feature completeness. Needs binary proof items.
- Metric/stat: use when a number is genuinely meaningful. Needs a source or reference that supports the number.
- Testimonial: use for user review, customer quote, or expert quote. Needs short quote copy and attribution.
- Privacy/trust: use for permissions, local-first, safety, or reliability. Needs settings, privacy, or account-state capture.
- Onboarding steps: use when first-run clarity matters. Needs sequential onboarding captures.
- Paywall/value: use when paid value must be shown. Needs locked and premium states when possible.
- Notification proof: use when reminders or alerts are part of the product. Needs notification or inbox state.
- Settings/depth: use when the app has serious controls. Needs settings, history, or detail screens.
- Multi-device: use when continuity matters. Needs two or more device/source variants.
- Landscape/desktop: use for web, tablet, or desktop product proof. Needs a wide source capture.

## Social And Marketing

- Launch poster: strong announcement with one hero source and bold brand treatment.
- Feature announcement: one feature, one visible proof state, clear release copy.
- Changelog/update: multiple small improvements or version highlights.
- Founder quote: short quote plus product visual.
- Case study: outcome, customer or user context, and product proof.
- Transparent device mock: device-only or website asset. Needs clean source and transparent export target.
- Square social proof: review, testimonial, or metric in a square target.
- Dark editorial: premium editorial mood, best for high-contrast UI.
- Bright brand splash: colorful announcement or launch asset.
- Minimal gallery: several UI states with restrained copy.

## Selection Checklist

1. Pick the card job first: promise, proof, workflow, depth, trust, conversion, or announcement.
2. Confirm the required source states exist.
3. Prefer templates that let visible UI prove the copy.
4. Avoid repeating the same screenshot crop across adjacent cards.
5. Use custom templates when the existing layout communicates well and should be reused.
